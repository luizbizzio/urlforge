How URL compression works

Can I compress from the address bar?

Put uf.do/ before a full URL. For example, enter uf.do/https://example.com/page to open a page with the compressed link ready to copy.

Where is the destination stored?

The compressed address lives in the part of the link after the #. URLForge restores it in the browser and opens it automatically, unless you enable Ask before opening.

Why is my link sometimes longer?

Repeated words and parameters tend to compress well. Very short URLs or random IDs have less room for savings. The result compares both addresses, including the short domain.

Which codec should I choose?

Leave it on Automatic to get the shortest version that preserves your URL exactly. Choose URLForge or ha.mr to try a specific codec.
