URLForge

Privacy

URLForge is designed to work locally in your browser.

Links and QR codes

The compressor form creates short links and QR codes in your browser. The destination is encoded in the part of the short link after the # and decoded by the browser when someone opens it.

Address bar shortcut

When you put uf.do/ before a URL, the destination up to its fragment is part of the request to our hosting provider. Paste sensitive links into the compressor form to keep them in your browser.

Browser preferences

Your Ask before opening setting is saved in your browser. The address bar shortcut keeps the current input in the tab's session history so you can reload the result.
