URLForge

Privacy

URLForge is designed to work locally in your browser.

URLs are not stored

The original URL, payload, and compressed link are not sent to or stored by the telemetry service.

Minimal technical metrics

When an installation enables optional telemetry, only technical counts and sizes are sent. They do not identify users.

Optional telemetry

Self-hosted installations can leave it disabled. Telemetry failures never block compression, QR generation, decoding, or redirects.
